# User guide

This guide has two parts: **Part 1 for employees** who vote, and **Part 2 for HR admins** who run contests. A short troubleshooting section is at the end.

---

# Part 1: Employees

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the portal link from the "Voting is open" email, or go to the portal address.
2. Choose how to sign in:
   - **Email code (no password needed).** Type your work email and press **Send me a code**. Check your inbox for a 6-digit code and type it in. The code works for 10 minutes. Use **Resend code** if it has not arrived (you can ask again after 30 seconds).
   - **Password.** If you have set a password, open the **Password** tab and sign in with your email and password.
3. You can only sign in if HR has marked you eligible. If you see "not on the eligible list", contact HR.

**Set a password (optional).** Click your initials at the top right, choose **Set a password**, and enter a new one (at least 10 characters, letters and numbers). **Sign out** is in the same menu.

**Light or dark.** The moon/sun button at the top changes the look. It cycles between matching your device, light and dark.

## 2. The home screen

At the top is a banner for each contest that is **open for voting**. If more than one is open, the banner slides between them by itself every few seconds. Use the arrows or dots to move yourself. It pauses while your mouse is over it.

Each slide shows:
- the **status** ("Voting open"), the region(s) it is open to, and how many entries there are;
- a **live countdown** (days, hours, minutes, seconds) to the closing time;
- **Start voting** (or **Continue voting** if you saved picks, or **See my vote** if you have voted) and **How it works**.

Below the banner are four tiles:
- **My ballot**: how many picks you have made, or "Submitted" once you have voted.
- **Results**: opens the results page ("Results aren't out yet" until HR publishes).
- **Rules**: opens the contest rules, scoring and tie-breaks.
- **Deadline**: when voting closes.

Further down:
- **Upcoming contests**: contests that are coming soon.
- **Past contests**: finished contests with the month and number of votes. Click one to see the top 3, and **your own vote** in it. Winners stay hidden until you click.

**Contests for other regions.** You can see every contest. If a contest is not open to your region you will see "Open to … · view only" and cannot vote in it. You can still read the results once published.

## 3. Voting

1. Press **Start voting**. You see all entries as cards. Each shows an entry number (for example *Entry 07*), a title and a short description. **You will not see who made it.**
2. **Tap or click a card** to open it large:
   - photos and posters show full size;
   - Word and PDF documents open in a viewer with the page count and **zoom**, labelled only "Entry 10.pdf";
   - videos play in the frame;
   - writing opens in a reading view.
   You can choose a place from inside the large view too.
3. Choose your places with the **1st / 2nd / 3rd** buttons on a card. You must choose **three different entries**. Picking a place that is already used moves it to the new entry. Press a selected button again to un-pick.
4. The bar at the bottom shows your three slots. Click a filled slot to remove that pick.
5. Not ready? Press **Save for later**. Your picks are waiting when you come back, and you can change them freely until you submit. **Saved picks that are never submitted before voting closes do not count.**
6. When all three slots are filled, press **Review**. Check the picks, then **Submit my vote**.
7. You see **"Thanks, your vote is in"** with your picks, and you get a confirmation email. **Votes are final.** You cannot vote again or change it.

**Points:** 1st place gives your entry 3 points, 2nd gives 2, 3rd gives 1.

**Time.** The countdown at the top of the voting page uses the server's clock. When it reaches zero the page tells you voting has closed and nothing more can be submitted.

**On a phone.** The voting page is built for phones: cards stack in one column and the ballot bar stays at the bottom.

## 4. After voting

- **See my vote:** from the home screen or the voting link you can always look at your picks.
- **Results:** once HR publishes, you get an email. The results page shows the **winners podium** with names, entries and points, **Your vote** (your three picks, where each finished, and a Winner badge if it won), and a table of **every other entry** with its number, description, competitor and points.

## 5. Privacy in short

- Nobody sees who made an entry until results are published.
- HR can see **that** you voted, never **who** you voted for.
- Voters cannot see running scores.

# Part 2: HR admins

Admins see a **HR admin** switch at the top. Click it to enter the admin area, and **Employee** to return to the employee view. The side menu has: **Overview**, **Contests**, **Users & regions**, **Email outbox**, **Activity log**.

## 1. Overview

Shows how many contests are open, how many are waiting for you to publish, drafts, and active employees; live turnout with a countdown for each open contest; recent activity; and quick links.

## 2. Create a contest

**Contests > New contest** (or **+ New contest**). Three steps:

1. **Basics**: name, theme, short description, rules (optional), and a banner image (optional).
2. **Entries and audience**:
   - **Entry types**: photo/poster, writing, document (PDF/Word), video. Choose more than one if you like.
   - **Who takes part**: **Regional** (one region), **Selected regions** (two or more), or **Global** (every region that is switched on). *Everyone can see the contest; only these regions can vote and get its emails.*
   - **Places to vote for**: default 3.
3. **Schedule**: **Voting ends** date and time, and the time zone (India time by default). Optionally switch on **Show as "Coming soon"** so employees see the name and theme before voting opens (entries stay hidden).

Press **Create contest**. You can change everything until you start it.

**Copy an earlier contest.** In the contest list, click the copy button on a row. You get a new draft with the same settings, region reach, email wording and banner, ready for new entries and a new end date.

## 3. Add entries

Open the contest and go to **Entries**. Employees send their work to HR outside the portal; you upload it here.

- **Add entry**: upload one file (or paste writing). Fill in:
  - **Title** and **Short description**. These are shown to voters. Do **not** name the entrant here.
  - **Competitor's name**. Saved against the entry and **hidden from voters everywhere** until you publish.
  - **Image description (alt text)** for photos, read aloud by screen readers.
- **Bulk upload**: drop many files at once. Each becomes one entry. File names are never used as titles (they may contain names). The portal then walks you through each entry that still needs a title, description and competitor name.
- **Edit** or **remove** an entry any time **before you start the contest**. After that, entries are locked.
- Each entry gets a permanent number (Entry 01, 02, ...). A removed entry's number is not reused.
- The portal automatically removes hidden file details (author, camera, GPS, company).
- **See it as an employee**: opens the real voting screen as employees will see it. Nothing you do there is saved or counted.

## 4. Start the contest

On the contest's **Overview**, the **Before you start** checklist shows what is still missing:
- name and description filled in;
- enough entries (at least the number of places, minimum 2);
- every entry has its file or text, a title and a description;
- every entry has a competitor name;
- the end date is set and in the future;
- the region has eligible employees.

When everything is ticked, press **Start contest**. You are told how many people will get the email. Voting opens **immediately** and the **Contest started** email goes to everyone in the participating regions. Nothing is emailed before this.

## 5. While voting is open

- **Live tracking** tab:
  - eligible employees, how many have voted, **turnout %**, and how many have **saved but not submitted**;
  - turnout by team and votes over time;
  - **Leaderboard** (HR only): each entry with its points, updating as votes arrive;
  - **Who has not voted yet**, with **Send reminder** (emails only people who have not voted);
  - **Voter list (Excel)** and **Results (Excel)** downloads.
- **Extend deadline**: set a later end. The portal and countdowns update straight away, and you can tick the box to email everyone the new time.
- **Close voting now**: ends voting immediately (with a confirmation). Unsubmitted saved picks do not count.
- Otherwise voting **closes by itself** at the end time. No action is needed.
- HR can see who has voted, but never who they voted for.

## 6. Check and publish the results

After voting closes, the contest appears under **Results to publish** on the Overview.

1. Open the **Results** tab. You see the winners, every entry with its 1st/2nd/3rd counts and points, and the competitor names. Ties: more 1st-place picks wins, then more 2nd-place picks, otherwise the place is shared.
2. Download the **Excel** results if you want to keep or share them.
3. Press **Publish results**. Employees can now see the winners and results, and the **Results declared** email goes to everyone in the participating regions. This cannot be undone.

Finished contests stay in the list as history.

## 7. Emails

Contest > **Emails**. There are five templates:
- **Contest started** (everyone in the regions, when you click Start)
- **Your vote is in** (the voter, right after they submit)
- **Results declared** (everyone in the regions, when you publish)
- **Reminder** (optional, people who have not voted)
- **Deadline extended** (optional)

For each one you can edit the **subject** and **message**, insert details with the `{{...}}` buttons (name, contest name, deadline, vote link, picks, winners, and so on), see a live **preview**, and **Send test to me** (goes only to you). **Reset wording** returns to the standard text. Edits apply to this contest only; copying a contest carries them over.

**Email outbox** (side menu) lists every email with its status (queued, sent, failed; in test mode "captured"). Click a subject to see the message.

## 8. Users and regions

**Users & regions** has two tabs.

**People**
- **Search and filter** by name, email, team, region, status and role.
- **Add person**: name, work email, team and **region (required)**, optionally HR admin.
- **Edit** a person, or use the **Active** switch to turn off someone who has left. Switched-off people cannot sign in or vote.
- **HR admin** switch gives or removes admin access. There must always be at least one active admin, and you cannot remove your own access.
- **Import spreadsheet**: upload `.xlsx` or `.csv` with columns **name, email, team, region** (region is required for every row). Download the template from the same dialog. You see a **check first** (new, to update, problem rows) before anything is saved. People are matched by email: existing ones are updated, new ones are added, and nobody is removed.

**Regions**
- Every person belongs to exactly one region.
- Switch a region on to make it available to contests. India is on by default. Add other regions with a short code and name.
- A region cannot be switched off while an unfinished contest uses it.

## 9. Activity log

Every admin action (contest created or changed, entries added or removed, contest started, deadline extended, voting closed, results published, downloads, users and regions changed) is recorded with **who** did it and **when**. It cannot be edited or deleted.

## 10. Quick launch checklist (under 30 minutes)

1. Users: make sure people and regions are up to date (import a spreadsheet if needed).
2. Create the contest (or copy last month's).
3. Upload entries and fill titles, descriptions, alt text and competitor names.
4. Set the end date and time.
5. **See it as an employee**, optionally send yourself a test email.
6. **Start contest**.
7. Watch **Live tracking**; send a **reminder** a day before the end.
8. After it closes, check the results and **Publish**.

# Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I did not get my code | Check spam, wait up to a minute, press Resend code. Only employees on the eligible list get one. |
| "Not on the eligible list" | Ask HR to check your email and that you are switched on. |
| I cannot vote in a contest | It may not be open to your region ("view only"), voting may not have started, or it has closed. |
| My saved picks vanished | Saved picks that were not submitted before voting closed do not count and are removed. |
| I voted by mistake | Votes are final by design. Ask HR if the rules allow an exception; the portal cannot change it. |
| (HR) Start contest is greyed out | Open the Overview tab and finish the unticked checklist items. |
| (HR) A region is missing in the contest form | Switch it on under Users & regions first. |
| (HR) A document will not upload | Use `.pdf` or `.docx` (not older `.doc`), not password-protected, under 100 MB. |
| (HR) Emails show "captured" | The portal is in test mode; set `MAIL_TRANSPORT=smtp` and SMTP settings to send real email. |
